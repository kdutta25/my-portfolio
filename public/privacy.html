Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers kaustubhdutta.com, the personal portfolio of Kaustubh Dutta, and the "Portfolio watch time" Google OAuth app that supplies the site's YouTube statistics.

Visitors to this site

The site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not ask you for personal information. It saves your theme (light or dark) and language choice in your browser's local storage so they persist between visits; that data never leaves your device.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and YouTube thumbnails and channel images are loaded from YouTube's image servers. Those services receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request, under Google's privacy policy.

The "Portfolio watch time" app

The site shows public statistics for the KausDiaries YouTube channel. To show the channel's total watch time, a build script signs in as the channel owner with read-only access to YouTube Analytics (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly).

  • It reads one figure: the channel's lifetime estimated minutes watched.
  • It accesses only the site owner's own channel. It is not offered to, and does not access, any other user's account.
  • The figure is published on this site as a rounded number of hours. No other analytics data is stored or shared.
  • The OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the site's GitHub repository and are used only by its build.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
